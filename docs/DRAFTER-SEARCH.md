# Drafter search: can we use someone else's trained drafter instead of training our own? (2026-09-29)

> Research only. No GPU was used. The only contact with the Sparks was a read-only `ls` of the head node's Hugging Face
> cache. Every acceptance number below is **quoted from its publisher**, from a stack other than ours, unless it is
> marked W11 (ours: `docs/RESULTS.md` W11 §6). Hugging Face was searched through its API: by name (`GLM-5.3-Flash`,
> `GLM-5.3`, `glm53`), by `base_model:` relation, by tag (`speculators`, `eagle3`, `eagle`, `dflash`, `dspark`, `mtp`,
> `draft`, `speculative-decoding`, `pard`, `medusa`) and by author (RedHatAI, incoai, canada-quant, modal-labs, zai-org,
> nvidia, local-inference-lab, LibertAIDAI, orcarouter, dealignai, drowzeys, neko-legends, mgoin, AlayaNeW, z-lab,
> Tencent, thoughtworks, vcruz305, turboderp). GitHub was searched with `gh` (repos, PRs, code) and ~30 GLM-5.3-Flash
> serving kits were read. The web search covered Reddit / X / vLLM / SGLang.
>
> Inputs from this repo: DRAFTER-TRAINING (§5.3 drafter table, §10 licences), DECODE-PLAN (T1-T4), RESULTS W11 §6,
> SFXNZ-AUDIT, EXPERIMENTS S4, `results/W11/`, the engine's `glm5_next/cuda/dflash2.py`, and the rigmark serving
> records in `~/.cache/rigmark/examples/serving-records/`.

## 0. Bottom line

- **Nobody has published a drafter that beats what we serve, and nobody has trained one for our target.**
  - Every trained GLM-5.3-Flash block drafter lands within ±1.5% of incoai's DFlash2 on the base model: canada-quant
    E / F / G, incoai's own two later checkpoints, and cfontes' re-fit. The family looks saturated at τ ≈ 3.6-3.7
    (K = 7, thinking-on mix).
  - **No distilled or self-distilled MTP head exists for GLM-5.3-Flash.** Every "-MTP" repo on the Hub is a quant or an
    extraction of the stock layer 45.
  - **No EAGLE-3, Medusa, PARD or Hydra head exists for GLM-5.3-Flash.** The nearest EAGLE-3 is for GLM-4.7-Flash, a
    different model.
  - **Nothing was trained on the abliterated weights.** The one abliteration-specific drafter (gorbatjovy's `fc`
    recalibration) was fitted to a different abliteration from ours: dealignai's layers 15-45 `o_proj` edit, where ours
    comes from orcarouter's whole-model projection. It would move our taps the wrong way.
- **So T1 (MTP self-distillation) stays exactly as planned.** No public head replaces it, and it is the lever with the
  largest published lift (FastMTP positions 2-3: 11 → 56% and 2 → 36%).
- **One candidate is worth a GPU hour: `modal-labs/GLM-5.3-Flash-DFlash`.**
  - It is **MIT**, and the same DFlash2 family (conv kernels + candidate selector).
  - It has 6 layers, 6 taps at layers 23 / 27 / 31 / 35 / 39 / 43, causal attention inside the block, and a 4,096-token
    window.
  - **It loads in our engine unchanged.** `dflash2.py` reads the taps, window, causality and rope θ from `config.json`.
  - **No acceptance number has been published anywhere**, so the expected gain is a prior of −10% to +10% τ on DFlash2
    rounds.
  - If it reaches parity, it gives two things incoai's drafter cannot:
    - a **shareable** drafter-enabled default for the public kit;
    - a **shareable T2 warm start** with no loader work.
- **DFlash2-G is not a drop-in win for speed.**
  - On the base model it gains +0.044 τ at K = 7 (+1.2%) and is 1.5% worse at K = 4. Our cost-derived depths sit nearer
    K = 3-4 on prose.
  - It has 8 layers instead of 5, which makes drafting ~1.6x more expensive.
  - Its full attention was trained on ≤ ~5k-token contexts, and its own authors report depth decay.
  - Its value is unchanged: an Apache-2.0 T2 warm start.
  - **Correction to DRAFTER-TRAINING §5.3:** its `embed_tokens` / `lm_head` are byte-identical to zai-org's (checked by
    range-reading three row blocks). The loader work shrinks to two items, **~1-1.5 days**: a capped window for
    `sliding_window: null` and `mask_embedding.pt`.
- **Two cheap levers came out of the search. Both are ours to run, not someone else's weights:**
  1. **An `fc`-only re-fit of incoai's drafter to our taps** (EXPERIMENTS S4, generalised). It costs hours of
     `train/dflash_distill.py` with everything but `fc` / `hidden_norm` frozen, on a 5-tap dump. That dump does not
     switch DFlash2 off while it records. It is private only (NC-ND).
     - head already holds `brandonmusic/GLM-5.3-Flash-tr3-4bpw`, a stock (not abliterated) EXL3 build.
     - If the engine loads it, the same prompts through stock and abliterated weights measure what abliteration costs
       the drafter, and that is the ceiling of this lever.
  2. **Open on-policy data** that can replace most of the 48 h generation step in DRAFTER-TRAINING §2.4:
     - `Zek-Takai/glm53-flash-harvest`: MIT, 246M tokens generated by base GLM-5.3-Flash (FP8), both thinking modes.
     - `shanjiaz/glm-5.3-flash-open-perfectblend-regen-25pct`: Red Hat's DSpark regeneration set. No licence on the
       card; the source prompts are Apache-2.0.
- **Recommendation:** run the §6 test (~3 h, one window) on modal-labs, with incoai `7d74cdd` (same-day baseline) and `bf582e4` as controls. Adopt
  nothing without its gate. Start T1 as planned, and feed the harvest data through `gendata.py tforce`. Choose T2's
  warm start after the test: modal-labs if it holds parity, otherwise DFlash2-G.

## 3. Compatibility with our engine

Our engine's `glm5_next/cuda/dflash2.py` reads these from the drafter's `config.json`:

- `target_layer_ids` (the taps `Engine.tap_rows` collects);
- `sliding_window` (0065 sizes the ring to `next_pow2(window + block + 65)`);
- `is_causal` (the attention kernel has a `CAUSAL` path);
- `rope_parameters.rope_theta`, `num_hidden_layers`, the heads and the MLP width.

It requires:

- two-tap convolutions;
- the selector;
- heads and the MLP that split over 2 ranks;
- a draft input from `embed(mask_token_id)` through the target's embedding;
- the target's head.

## 4. Ranking for our prose / code / agent mix

Which rounds a swap touches:

- A DFlash2-family swap changes only DFlash2 rounds. Under the production policy (W11 §6) those are greedy rounds only:
  ~45% of greedy prose rounds, ~63% of code rounds and ~42% of agent rounds.
- Sampled requests draft with MTP only, so a swap leaves them unchanged.
- "Gain" is the change in tokens a DFlash2 round, net of drafting cost.

## 5. Recommendation

1. **Run §6 in one window (~3 h).**
   - Arm: modal-labs.
   - Controls: incoai `7d74cdd` (production, and W11's paired baseline) and incoai `bf582e4e`.
   - cfontes TR3-v3 is optional: +25 min for a curiosity arm.
2. **Adopt modal-labs only if all of these hold:**
   - untruncated prose p1..p4 ≥ incoai's −0.01 at every position;
   - code / agent tokens a DFlash2 round ≥ incoai's −3%;
   - the production-policy greedy tok/s is not lower on any class;
   - exact 10/10 and batchexact 4/4 pass.

   If it passes, it also becomes the public kit's default drafter (MIT) and the T2 warm start. That replaces the
   DFlash2-G loader work, 2-3 days in DRAFTER-TRAINING §5.3 and now ~1-1.5 days.
3. **Before T2, run an `fc`-only stage** (hours, private).
   - Freeze everything in `train/dflash_distill.py` except `fc` and `hidden_norm`.
   - Train on a 5-tap dump (`GLM53_TF_DRAFT_DUMP_TAPS=fp8`, the served tap set, so DFlash2 keeps drafting while it
     records).
   - Optional ceiling: if the engine loads `brandonmusic/GLM-5.3-Flash-tr3-4bpw` (on the head node, the stock base in EXL3
     4 bpw), run the same W11 acceptfix prompts on it. The gap to our abliterated target is the most any drift fix can
     recover.
4. **T1 unchanged; shorten its data step.**

## 6. GPU test: modal-labs vs incoai, acceptance by position (offline-first, production restored after)

The engine is exact: the drafter never changes a reply. With the same prompts, the same config and greedy / keyed
sampling, every arm produces **byte-identical replies**. Each request's `tf.sha256` must match W11's
`results/W11/acceptfix-f7.jsonl`, so the per-prompt acceptance comparison is perfectly paired.

```bash
# 0. both nodes, while prod still serves (downloads only)
for h in <head-ssh> "$WORKER_SSH"; do ssh $h '
  hf download modal-labs/GLM-5.3-Flash-DFlash   --revision dae6d319510889a7f1ac5242d5b7fb2d5d95eb05
  hf download incoai/GLM-5.3-Flash-DFlash2      --revision bf582e4eacc1810f76656d1811693ff6c6737d2a
  # optional arm: hf download cfontes/GLM-5.3-Flash-DFlash2-TR3-v3 --revision 45f7e139584e299f9850c9f92df2c4563a470432
'; done

# 1. window (lease + watchdog as in W11); per arm, on the head:
S=/root/.cache/huggingface/hub; mkdir -p results/DS
for arm in inco-7d7:$S/models--incoai--GLM-5.3-Flash-DFlash2/snapshots/7d74cdd881ed7e32c31175984a67823127b66cfe \
           modal:$S/models--modal-labs--GLM-5.3-Flash-DFlash/snapshots/dae6d319510889a7f1ac5242d5b7fb2d5d95eb05 \
           inco-bf5:$S/models--incoai--GLM-5.3-Flash-DFlash2/snapshots/bf582e4eacc1810f76656d1811693ff6c6737d2a; do
  name=${arm%%:*}; d=${arm#*:}
  DRAFTER=$d scripts/serve.sh restart            # prod.env otherwise; first start writes the drafter's prepared folder
  scripts/serve.sh canary                        # the count probe must keep TPR >= 1.3 (a wrong tap convention collapses it)
  python3 results/W11/w11req.py acceptfix results/DS/acceptfix-$name.jsonl   # tf_policy "4" (MTP control) and "f7"
  python3 results/W11/w11req.py accept    results/DS/accept-$name.jsonl      # production policy, greedy + sampled
  python3 results/W11/accept.py results/DS/acceptfix-$name.jsonl --json results/DS/acceptfix-$name.json
  python3 results/W11/accept.py results/DS/accept-$name.jsonl    --json results/DS/accept-$name.json
  python3 bench/acceptpos.py results/DS/acceptfix-inco-7d7.jsonl results/DS/acceptfix-$name.jsonl   # B - A per position
done
# 2. exactness on the winning arm only: the usual exact 10/10 + batchexact 4/4 (tests/cuda), same DRAFTER
# 3. restore: scripts/serve.sh restart with config/prod.env unchanged; canary; watchdog tick; lease released
```

Read the results as follows.

- **Hygiene.**
  - Every arm's `tf.sha256` must equal the `inco-7d7` arm's for the same (prompt, temperature, policy). If not, stop:
    something other than the drafter changed. They should also equal W11's `acceptfix-f7.jsonl` unless the image
    changed since W11.
  - The `m` (MTP x4) rows must reproduce W11's MTP table within ±0.01.
- **Decision.** Use `acceptfix-*.json` rows `prose|all|f`, `code|all|f`, `agent|all|f`: `p_cum[0..3]` and
  `tokens_a_round`. Apply the gates of §5 item 2. Then check the `accept-*` production-policy tok/s per class.
- **Drafting cost.** `round_kinds.draft_ms` / rounds, modal vs incoai. The expectation is ≤ +20%.
- **Size of the evidence.** W11's set is ~1.4k prose DFlash2 rounds per arm, so a position-1 difference below ~0.03 is
  noise.
- **Second stage, offline on held-out data** (when the DRAFTER-TRAINING data window runs). Dump the union tap set once:

  ```
  GLM53_TF_DRAFT_DUMP_TAP_LAYERS=5,9,14,19,23,24,27,28,31,33,35,38,39,42,43
  ```

  That covers incoai, G and modal: 15 taps, ~70 KB a token with fp8 taps (61 KB of taps plus the 8.4 KB record), so a 300k-token held-out set is ~21 GB. DFlash2 is
  off while the dump runs. Then:

  ```bash
  python3 train/eval_accept.py --model $MODEL --dumps /data/dump/* --dflash $INCOAI --out eval-incoai.json
  python3 train/eval_accept.py --model $MODEL --dumps /data/dump/* --dflash $MODAL  --out eval-modal.json
  python3 train/eval_accept.py --model $MODEL --dumps /data/dump/* --dflash $DFLASH2G --out eval-g.json  # no engine loader needed offline
  ```

  - Compare `greedy` / `T=0.6` / `T=1.0` a_k per row kind (`d` decode rows, `p` prompt rows) and past the 2,051-token
    position. The last split shows G's long-context behaviour before any loader work.
  - Held-out documents are 2% by conversation, so this set is ~100x W11's. It decides T2's warm start.

Budget: ~20 min a start (the first with a new drafter writes its prepared folder) plus ~25 min of requests a
arm (`acceptfix` + `accept`). **~3 h for three arms plus the restore.** The DSpark and G arms are not in this window:
neither loads without code.
